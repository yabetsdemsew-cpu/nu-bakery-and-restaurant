About Us

Welcome to NU Bakery and Restaurant, a premium family destination located in the heart of Addis Ababa. We pride ourselves on offering a harmonious blend of freshly baked pastries, artisanal coffee, and a diverse culinary menu ranging from authentic Ethiopian dishes to international favorites. Our elegant atmosphere is designed to turn every meal into a memorable moment.
